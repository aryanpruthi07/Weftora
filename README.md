## 🏭 About Weftora Textiles

**Weftora** is a premier textile manufacturing and export enterprise based in the historic textile capital of **Panipat, Haryana, India**. We specialize in high-absorbency, skin-gentle bath and personal linen engineered for newborns, children, women, and modern hospitality environments.

We supply retail store chains, apparel boutiques, e-commerce brands (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho), and regional distributors across India and international markets with direct factory pricing, flexible low MOQs, and OEM private labeling.
